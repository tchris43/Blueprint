## Why it matters
Every scene: the tags on boxes ('Building · Alice', 'Not in plan') and the card text are what the audience actually reads.

## What to do
- List every piece of on-screen text the boards use (status tags, legend, card headings, buttons) in `docs/copy.md`.
- For each, write the plain version and one alternative. Keep tags under 20 characters.
- Board owners pick and apply them.

## Done when

- [ ] Board owners have signed off
