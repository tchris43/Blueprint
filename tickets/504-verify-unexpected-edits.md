# 504 · Try 5 unexpected edits and record what Blueprint does

**Labels:** `starter` · `demo-shop` · `verify`  
**Depends on:** [101](101-post-hook-real-edits.md), [202](202-conflict-to-nodes.md)  
**Owner:** _unclaimed_

## Why it matters
Judges will poke at the edges. We want to know the answers before they ask.

## What to do
- In a demo-shop clone with the hooks on, try at least: renaming a file; both people editing the same file; a change that breaks tests but not types; deleting a function someone else calls; editing a test file only.
- For each, record in `docs/verify/unexpected-edits.md`: what you did, what the board showed, what you expected, and whether it's a problem.

## Done when
- [ ] 5+ cases recorded
- [ ] Anything that looks like a bug is filed as a new ticket
- [ ] Show the team the result and explain how it works in 2 minutes. You present this piece at the final demo.

## Stretch
Find a case where Blueprint stays quiet but should have spoken up.
