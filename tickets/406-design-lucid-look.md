## Why it matters
Every scene: judges see the board for the whole demo. It has to read instantly from the back of a room.

## What to do
- Make two versions of scene 4 in Lucid (colors, borders, spacing, legend). Change only the values in `styleFor` / layout constants.
- Screenshot both at projector size (zoomed out, from across the room).
- Post both to the team; the team picks.

## Done when
- [ ] Two screenshots posted, a pick made, the winner committed
- [ ] Red vs green is distinguishable for colorblind viewers (check with a simulator)
- [ ] Show the team the result and explain how it works in 2 minutes. You present this piece at the final demo.

## Stretch
Pick a palette that also works on Lucid's dark mode.
