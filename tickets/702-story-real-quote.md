# 702 · Get a real quote: 'How do you find out what devs actually built?'

**Labels:** `starter` · `demo-pitch` · `story`  
**Depends on:** nothing  
**Owner:** _unclaimed_

## Why it matters
The pitch opener. One real person's answer beats any statistic we could invent.

## What to do
- Ask a PM, or a Lucid engineer at the event: 'How do you find out what developers actually built, and when do surprises show up?'
- Write their answer word for word in `docs/pitch-outline.md`, and get permission to quote it (name or role).

## Done when
- [ ] At least one real, permitted quote
- [ ] Show the team the result and explain how it works in 2 minutes. You present this piece at the final demo.

## Stretch
Ask a second person and pick the stronger quote.
