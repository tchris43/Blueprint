# 051 · Test Priya's decision panel on a real non-technical person

**Labels:** `starter` · `demo-pitch` · `design`  
**Depends on:** [024](024-lucid-panel-decision.md)  
**Owner:** _unclaimed_

## Why it matters
Scenes 4–5 claim a PM can settle this alone. We should find out if that's true before the judges do.

## What to do
- Find one non-technical person (friend, roommate, family). Show them scene 4 on the board with no explanation.
- Ask: 'What is this asking you? What would you pick, and why?'
- Write down, word for word, what confused them, in `docs/verify/priya-test.md`.

## Done when
- [ ] Notes from one real session
- [ ] 2–3 concrete wording or layout changes proposed to the board owners
- [ ] Show the team the result and explain how it works in 2 minutes. You present this piece at the final demo.

## Stretch
Test a second person after the changes and compare.
